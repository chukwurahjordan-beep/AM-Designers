What we produce

Documentation built for the people who install and maintain the system.

DWG.01

Zone Diagrams

Clear, correctly scaled zone plans showing device locations, zone boundaries, and panel indication — ready for the panel cabinet and the O&M manual.

DWG.02

Cause & Effect Schedules

Full cause and effect matrices covering detection, sounders, dampers, and interfaces — written so commissioning engineers can programme straight from the document.

DWG.03

As-Built Documentation

Site-verified drawings updated to reflect what was actually installed, not just what was originally designed — the version that holds up at handover and at the next service visit.

DWG.04

Compliance Review

An independent check of existing drawings against BS 5839 before they go to building control or a client sign-off, with issues flagged and corrected.

Built against the standard

Every drawing is checked before it's issued, not after it's queried.

Late-stage corrections cost installers time and cost you the relationship. Drawings are reviewed against the relevant standard before issue, so what lands on site is what gets signed off.

BS 5839-1

Fire detection and alarm systems for buildings — code of practice for system design, installation, and servicing.

BS 5839-6

Referenced for domestic and residential premises where a lower category system applies.

ZONE & C&E

Zone plans and cause & effect documents cross-checked against each other before issue, so the panel programming and the wall-mounted diagram always agree.

How a drawing gets issued

From site information to issued-for-construction.

Step one

Information in

We take your site survey, panel schedule, or existing drawings — whatever you already have.

Step two

Draft

Zone diagram and/or cause & effect drafted to scale, referenced against BS 5839.

Step three

Review

You check it against the site. We revise until it matches what's actually there.

Step four

Issue

Final PDF and source file delivered, ready for the panel cabinet or the O&M file.
